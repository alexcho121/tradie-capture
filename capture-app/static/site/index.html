On site: one button, then back to work

  1. 1

    Tap the big button

    It’s sized for a gloved thumb, and it’s the only thing to press.

  2. 2

    Say what you were asked

    Who asked, what changed, where, and any extra time. Talk like you’d tell a mate, then tap again. It stops by itself at one minute.

  3. 3

    Hold the phone up

    It counts 3, 2, 1 and takes the photo. No extra button to find.

  4. 4

    Saved

    The time and your location are added for you. No signal? It saves on the phone and uploads later.

Later: a written record, from your words only

When you’ve got a minute, open the record. AI turns your voice note into the written version: who asked, what changed, where, the extra time and materials. You read it, fix anything that’s off, and it’s done.

  • Not mentioned

    It doesn’t fill gaps

    If you didn’t say it, it stays blank. It never guesses a price, and 300 mil doesn’t turn into $300. Every value is checked against the transcript of your recording before you see it. Speech-to-text can still mishear a word, so read it through.

  • Check

    It tells you when it isn’t sure

    Say “probably two hours” and the time is marked Check, so you look at it before anyone else does.

  • Original, kept unchanged

    Your evidence stays as it was

    Editing the written record never changes the voice note or the photo, and every edit is logged.

Built for the way a job site works

  • Works without signal

    Captures save on your phone first and upload when you’re back in range.

  • Nothing to type on site

    One button. Things the phone already knows, like the time and where you are, are filled in for you.

  • Private to your browser

    No account needed. Your records are tied to the browser you made them in, and other visitors can’t open them. Voice notes are sent to OpenAI to be transcribed.

  • Short on purpose

    Recordings stop at one minute. It’s a note about one job, not a meeting recording.

What works today, and what doesn’t yet

Works today

  • One big button: voice note, photo, time and location
  • Saving with no signal, then uploading automatically
  • Speech-to-text and an AI draft, checked against what you said
  • Editing the draft, with every change logged
  • Records that only your browser can open

Not yet

  • Accounts, or the same records on two phones
  • Sending the record to the builder to confirm
  • Reminders for contract notice deadlines
  • Testing with working tradies on real sites

This is a hackathon demo. Saved records can be cleared at any time, so please don’t rely on it for real jobs yet. The example on this page is made up.

“About 15 seconds” is the median time from first tap to the photo across 13 test captures by our own team (13.7 seconds). We haven’t timed working tradies yet.

Tradie Capture keeps a record of what you captured and when. It isn’t legal advice, and it doesn’t guarantee you’ll be paid.
